# V2.4.3a-r2 MarketChart Paint Health Exposure

Observability-only patch. No Decision Engine or Paper Trade behavior changes.

- Adds `MarketChart.paint_health_snapshot()`.
- Exposes invalid-value, reentrant-paint and paint-failure counters in heartbeat, snapshots and runtime metrics.
- Copies `last_issue` into a serialization-safe dictionary.
- Performs no file I/O and takes no blocking lock inside `paintEvent()`.
- RuntimeTelemetry Qt timers collect the snapshot on the GUI thread.
